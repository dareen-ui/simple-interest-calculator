# Simple Interest Calculator

A calculator that calculates simple interest given principal, annual rate of interest and time period in years.

## Formula

```
Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100
```

**Where:**

- **P** = Principal amount
- **R** = Annual rate of interest (in %)
- **T** = Time period in years

## Example

If you borrow a principal of **10,000** at an annual interest rate of **5%** for **3 years**:

```
Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 3) / 100 = 1500
```

The total amount to be paid back is **10,000 + 1,500 = 11,500**.

## How to Use

1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual rate of interest.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. The calculator displays the simple interest.
